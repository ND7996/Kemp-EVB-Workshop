# Slide notes

1. Classical MD samples motion with fixed bonds; QM/MM treats a reacting region quantum mechanically; EVB couples reactant and product descriptions.
2. Asp162 OD1 accepts Ht while the substrate ring opens.
3. Reactant and product are descriptions of the same atoms with different bonds.
4. The EVB table combines the two state energies. α shifts product energy; H₁₂ controls mixing.
5. A full calculation prepares states, relaxes them, samples FEP windows, and analyzes the profile.
6. Use the saved viewers to explore the model and profile.
7. The paper Q6 barrier and short OpenMM profile differ in model setup and sampling.
8. The local five-atom example runs on Linux with a CPU; it is not a free-energy calculation.
