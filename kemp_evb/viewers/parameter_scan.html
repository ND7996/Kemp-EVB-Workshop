WORKSHOP SENSITIVITY / TARGET MATCHING — NOT A PREDICTION. Curves reuse the saved energies. Matching one barrier does not identify both parameters or establish convergence. Reaction free energies are shown too.

Reading the plot: grey dashed = archived-parameter baseline; blue = selected example; red dashed = chosen barrier target. Both curves reuse the same saved OpenMM energies; their coupling/offset parameters differ. The marked profile maximum is a binned TS estimate, not a validated molecular transition state. Raw gap zero means equal unshifted state energies; offset-corrected gap zero means equal offset-corrected state energies. Neither equality guarantees a free-energy maximum. The peak need not lie at zero on either physical coordinate.

Coordinates and energies are unchanged. Missing bins remain gaps.

Each curve uses its reactant minimum as zero where resolved; otherwise its global minimum is used and no barrier is assigned. The centring option subtracts the selected peak's original raw gap from both curves. It changes only the displayed origin; it does not move atoms, fit energies, change barriers or prove that a molecular TS has zero energy gap.
