Why run independent replicas?

SEM measures estimated precision of this shortened-protocol mean, not accuracy versus the paper. n counts complete independently seeded calculations, not lambda windows or saved frames. With only two replicas it is highly uncertain; shared starting-state/model bias is not captured. Zero spread of rounded barriers would not imply zero physical uncertainty. Published ±0.6 is SD, not SEM. A ±SEM error bar is not a 95% confidence interval.

Both runs use the same shortened reconstruction, with independently seeded initial velocities. Neither replaces the published30replicas or long equilibration/production. A small SEM can coexist with a biased mean.

Paper: Listov et al., Nature2025, Supplementary Table3
